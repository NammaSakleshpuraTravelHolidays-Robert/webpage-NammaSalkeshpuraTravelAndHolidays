## Coverage and Naming Caveats

- Both images/ and images_tripData/ were inspected. The new section uses appropriate images from both folders.
- The Scenic carousel contains five matching local photos; Offbeat contains three. Both card and route-detail popup carousels use these complete collections. Card frames fill their available area; popup photos retain their full frame. Photos advance automatically every 1.8 seconds while visible, including on hover/focus, without play/pause buttons. Offscreen carousels and hidden browser tabs suspend their timers. Reduced-motion preferences remove the sliding transition without stopping automatic image changes.
- No suitable local destination photographs were identified for the Heritage, Pilgrimage, Coorg or Chikkamagaluru routes. Those four cards are text-first rather than illustrated with misleading destination imagery. All six routes remain available. The requirement for a matching photograph on every route card is not fully met.
- Supplied photo labels are not independent verification of a destination, authenticity or reuse permission.
- Route copy preserves the implementation prompt's names. Photo labels or filenames vary: Manjehalli / Magajahalli, Mukanamane / Mookanamane, Mallalli / Mallahalli, Hosahalli / Hoshalli, Bisle / Bisile. Confirm identity before using ambiguous photos for individual stops.
- The Mankanahalli photo contains questionable Arabian Sea / Bay of Bengal text and is not used. Prasanna Rameshwara Temple is not substituted for any temple in the supplied routes. Other originals remain available and unchanged.
- No additional image search or acquisition was needed for the implemented local-photo fallback. Any future image acquisition must use the VS Code integrated browser only and record the original URL, creator, permission/license and required attribution here.
